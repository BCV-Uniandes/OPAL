## Models

Energy metrics use the ORB, MACE and UMA interatomic potentials through [LeMat-GenBench](https://github.com/LeMaterial/lemat-genbench). Their weights are downloaded by the user at run time and are not distributed with this repository; each is subject to its own license and terms of use. UMA is gated on Hugging Face and requires accepting its license at https://huggingface.co/facebook/UMA.

## Data

The data release on [OSF](https://osf.io/9jhm4/) contains:

- `gt_files/` and `poly_files/`: ground-truth test structures and polymorph groups derived from the test splits of the four datasets below, as distributed with [CDVAE](https://github.com/txie-93/cdvae) and [DiffCSP](https://github.com/jiaor17/DiffCSP).
- `pred_files/`: structures that we generated by retraining and sampling each evaluated method with its official implementation: CDVAE, DiffCSP, MatterGen, CrystalFlow, FlowMM, FlowLLM, DiffCSP++ and SGEquiDiff (the latter two with ground-truth and CSPML templates), plus OPAL-CSP. Please cite the original papers of these methods when using their predictions.
- `results/`: precomputed alignments, intersection sets and metric values produced by this code.

The underlying structures come from:

| Dataset | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `carbon_24` | Pickard, *AIRSS data for carbon at 10 GPa and the C+N+H+O system at 1 GPa*, Materials Cloud, 2020 | CC BY 4.0 |
| `mp_20`, `mpts_52` | Jain et al., *The Materials Project: A materials genome approach to accelerating materials innovation*, APL Materials 1(1):011002, 2013 | CC BY 4.0 |

If you use these datasets, please cite the original sources above.
